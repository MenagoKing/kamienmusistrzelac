1. Who we are

The site is operated by Space Rocks Ltd., a company registered in the British Virgin Islands under company number 2087463, with its registered office at Craigmuir Chambers, Road Town, Tortola, VG1110, British Virgin Islands ("Space Rocks", "we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

2. Data we collect

Data you give us

  • Account: callsign (username), email address, password (stored only as a salted hash), avatar choice.
  • Sign in with Google: if you choose it, your Google account ID, name and email address as shared by Google.
  • Shipping (when you redeem a specimen): full name, postal address, email, and optionally phone number.
  • Linked wallet: a Solana wallet address you choose to link for withdrawals.
  • Waitlist: your email address.
  • Messages: anything you send us when you contact support.

Data created when you use the site

  • Your Space Rocks wallet: the Solana address created for your account, and its balances.
  • Activity: purchases, reservations, pack openings, listings, sales, redemptions, refunds and payouts, with amounts and transaction signatures.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, and logs needed to run and secure the site.

We do not collect payment card details. Payments are made in USDC or SOL from your wallet on the Solana blockchain.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and run your account and your Space Rocks wallet.
  • To process purchases, reservations, escrow, refunds, buybacks and payouts, and to mint, transfer and burn your NFTs.
  • To ship a specimen when you redeem it, including passing your address to the dealer who ships it.
  • To keep the site secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and meet legal, tax, accounting and sanctions-screening obligations.
  • To answer your questions and send service messages (for example, a tracking number or a refund notice).
  • To send news about launches and drops, only if you joined the waitlist or agreed to it. You can unsubscribe at any time.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. Blockchain and wallets

Your NFTs, wallet address and transactions are recorded on the Solana blockchain, which is public and permanent. Anyone can see a wallet's address, balances and transaction history. We cannot change or delete data once it is on the blockchain. We do not publish your name, email or address on-chain.

Your Space Rocks wallet's private key is generated when you create your account and stored on our servers, encrypted. We use it only to carry out actions you request (payments, transfers) and never share it. You can export it from your account; once exported, keeping it safe is your responsibility.

6. Who we share your data with

  • Independent dealers who hold and ship specimens: your name, shipping address and contact details, only when you redeem. A dealer is named to you after redemption.
  • Service providers acting on our instructions: hosting and servers, Solana network infrastructure (RPC providers), email delivery, shipping and insurance carriers.
  • Google, if you use Sign in with Google, under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Authorities, advisers and auditors where the law requires it or to protect our rights, our users or the public.
  • A buyer or successor if our business is sold or merged, under the same protections.

7. International transfers

We and our providers may process data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom. Where required, we protect such transfers with appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

8. How long we keep your data

  • Account data: while your account is open, and up to 24 months after you close it.
  • Purchase, payment and shipping records: up to 7 years, for accounting, tax and dispute purposes.
  • Technical logs: up to 90 days, unless needed to investigate a security incident.
  • Waitlist: until you unsubscribe.

Data recorded on the blockchain cannot be removed by us.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest for wallet keys, hashed passwords, access controls and monitoring. No system is perfectly secure: please use a strong, unique password and keep any exported private key offline.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, receive a copy in a portable format, and withdraw consent. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. To exercise a right, contact us at the address below; we may need to verify your identity and will reply within one month.

11. Cookies

We use only cookies that the site needs to work: a sign-in cookie that keeps you logged in, and a separate one for administrators. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Your browser may also store small settings locally (for example, your last chosen tab).

12. Children

Space Rocks is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page with a new date, and tell you by email or on the site if the changes are significant.
