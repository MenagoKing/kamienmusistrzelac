§07Questions

Before you buy.

Is every specimen a real meteorite?

Yes. Each one has an official name and classification listed in the Meteoritical Bulletin, a weigh-in on video, a certificate, and provenance and export papers. No file, no listing.

Is the market random?

No. Every listed specimen has one fixed price, set from its documented purchase price. You see the exact rock you buy before you pay.

What can a pack be worth?

Every pack lands in a band. Floor: 70 to 85% of the pack price in vault value, never below 70%. Par: 90 to 120%. Hit: 150% or more. Each sealed series of 100 holds exactly 78, 19 and 3, published before sale opens, with live counts of what’s left.

What happens to a rock I sell back?

You get 85% of its vault value in USDC, instantly. The rock goes into the next unopened series, never the one on sale, so every published count stays exact.

Can I get the actual rock?

Yes. Burn the NFT and we ship the sealed capsule to you, insured, to the countries we can ship to. Shipping is charged at cost plus a handling fee.

What happens if a launch is scrubbed?

The locked specimens roll over to the next drop, so it grows. Packs you opened stay yours.

What is $ROCKS for?

Early access to launch drops, and a buyback and burn funded by pack sales. It is not a claim on the vault or on any rock.
